# Asset provenance and redistribution notes

The following assets are intentionally retained in the application at the
project owner's request. They are **excluded from the project's MIT license**;
that license does not grant additional rights in these files.

- `trajectory_lab/ui/resources/branding/trajectorylab_logo.svg` is the vector
  logo supplied by the project owner. The corresponding PNG is used in the
  About dialog and documentation. Its embedded provenance identifies Recraft.
  The project owner must confirm the Recraft plan and terms that applied when
  the asset was generated before public redistribution. Recraft's current
  Terms of Service (effective 21 October 2026) state that Free Tier Assets are
  owned by Recraft and restricted to personal use; paid-plan assets are treated
  differently. See https://www.recraft.ai/legal/terms and
  https://www.recraft.ai/pricing.
- `trajectory_lab/ui/resources/cow.png` is the photograph supplied by the
  project owner for the About-window easter egg. The project has not
  independently verified the photograph's copyright or redistribution grant.
  The project owner must confirm that they own the relevant rights or have
  permission to redistribute it publicly.
- `trajectory_lab/ui/resources/trajectorylab.png` and
  `trajectory_lab/ui/resources/trajectorylab.ico` are the compact application
  icon assets. Confirm their provenance before redistribution if they are
  based on third-party artwork.

Do not interpret the presence of these files in the repository or Windows
package as a license grant. If the owner cannot confirm redistribution rights,
remove or replace the affected asset before publishing the public repository
or binary release.
